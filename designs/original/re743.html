Introduction & Scope of Inspection

At the formal request of Mr. Pradeepkumar, the specialist engineering team of Rukn El-Tatawer for Water Leak Detection, Treatment & Building Maintenance LLC conducted a comprehensive technical inspection of the residential apartment located at Al Maha Tower 5, Unit 1401, Reem Island, Abu Dhabi, on 17, 07, 2026.

This report aims to accurately diagnose all water leakage and structural moisture issues within the unit, identify root causes, assess current and potential future damage, and present engineering solutions ranked by effectiveness, cost, and time efficiency. The inspection covered 3 bathrooms — including floor tiling systems, drainage points, and sub-base layers — as well as visible wall humidity and moisture penetration across the apartment's internal and external walls.

General Condition Assessment

Bathrooms (3 Units)
All three bathrooms exhibit multiple failures within the waterproofing system at floor and wall interfaces. Active water migration through degraded grout joints was confirmed, with water penetrating the sub-base layer and migrating to adjacent areas.
Wall Moisture & Humidity
Visible moisture and rising damp were identified on multiple internal and external-facing walls across the apartment. The plaster layer has deteriorated in several zones, and repainting alone will not resolve the underlying moisture ingress without structural treatment.
Overall Assessment
The combined effect of bathroom leakage and wall moisture has created a persistent damp environment within the unit, which — if left untreated — poses significant risks to both structural integrity and occupant health.

Technical Findings

A. BATHROOM FLOOR & WATERPROOFING ISSUES (3 Bathrooms)

  • 1
    Non-Waterproof Grout Used in Tile Joints
    Standard non-waterproof grout was applied during the original tiling installation — a material unsuitable for wet zones. This rendered the joint system entirely permeable to water under daily usage conditions.
    High Severity
  • 2
    Salinity Reaction & Accelerated Joint Erosion
    Prolonged exposure to water containing dissolved salts triggered a progressive chemical reaction with the grout matrix, resulting in complete erosion of tile joints across all three bathrooms — creating open pathways for direct water infiltration into the sub-base layer.
    High Severity
  • 3
    Sub-base Settlement & Void Formation
    Water saturation of the sand sub-base layer over an extended period caused progressive settlement, resulting in air voids beneath the ceramic tiles. These voids undermine the structural support of the tile bed and accelerate overall floor system degradation.
    High Severity
  • 4
    Active Water Leakage & Saturated Sub-base
    Water infiltrating through degraded joints has fully saturated the sub-base fill. Active leakage was confirmed below the bathroom floors, with moisture migration extending to adjacent areas and neighbouring units below.
    High Severity
  • 5
    Drainage Deficiency at Drain Points
    Reduced drainage efficiency was noted at the floor drain outlets across the inspected bathrooms. Partial blockage or insufficient gradient may be contributing to water pooling and prolonged surface contact — accelerating tile joint deterioration.
    Medium Severity

Moisture Infiltration — Internal & External Walls
  • Visible damp patches and moisture staining identified on multiple internal walls throughout the apartment

  • Discolouration and efflorescence observed at wall-floor junctions, consistent with rising damp and salt migration
  • The existing paint and plaster layers are providing no meaningful moisture barrier — repainting without structural treatment will be cosmetic only and temporary

Root Cause Analysis

1
Inappropriate Material Selection During Construction
Standard-grade, non-waterproof grout was applied in wet zones, contravening basic engineering requirements for bathroom environments in the Gulf's high-salinity climate. This was the primary trigger for all subsequent failures.
2
Absence of Primary Waterproofing Membrane
No effective waterproofing layer was applied beneath the ceramic tile system or at sub-base level during original construction, leaving the structural slab fully exposed to moisture absorption over time.
3
Cumulative Damage from Delayed Treatment
The leakage did not develop suddenly — it accumulated progressively over time without corrective intervention, allowing water to fully saturate the sub-base layers, create settlement voids, and ultimately penetrate surrounding walls.
4
Climatic & Salinity Factors
Abu Dhabi's high ambient temperature and elevated water salinity significantly accelerate the chemical degradation of non-waterproof grout, leading to a faster erosion cycle than would occur in temperate climates.
5
Inadequate Wall Waterproofing at Construction Stage
The absence of a moisture-resistant plaster system behind the wall finish allowed atmospheric humidity and bathroom steam to penetrate the plaster over time, resulting in the observed rising damp and surface deterioration.

Final Recommendations

Engineering Team Recommendations — Rukn El-Tatawer

Based on the findings of this comprehensive field inspection and root cause analysis, the engineering team of Rukn El-Tatawer recommends adopting Solution 2 (Injection System) as the primary and optimal treatment method. Upon client approval, our team will proceed as follows:

The bathroom leakage issues documented in this report represent an active and ongoing structural risk. Water continues to migrate beyond the bathroom boundaries with every day of use, penetrating the concrete slab, saturating adjacent walls, and potentially reaching the apartment directly below. In multi-storey residential buildings, this constitutes a direct liability to neighbouring units and to the building structure as a whole.

Continued delay in treatment may result in damage to the apartment below, for which the property owner of Unit 1401 may bear full legal and financial responsibility under UAE building regulations and civil liability law. The longer treatment is deferred, the greater the structural deterioration — and the significantly higher the eventual cost of remediation.

Confirmed Finding — Lower Apartment Ceiling Already Affected: It is with absolute certainty that the moisture now visible on the walls of Unit 1401 did not begin here. At present, that ceiling moisture is concealed beneath gypsum board and decorative finishes — masking the true extent of the damage. However, this concealment is only temporary. As water saturation continues to build, it is only a matter of time before the ceiling plasterwork, gypsum, and decorative finishes of the lower unit begin to deteriorate, crack, and collapse — at which point the owner of Unit 1401 will face an immediate and undeniable legal and financial claim from the affected neighbours below.
